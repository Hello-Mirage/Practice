## Hackathon judging checklist

- **Problem clarity:** Can someone understand the problem quickly?
- **Relevance:** Is the problem meaningful to the intended users?
- **Solution fit:** Does the MVP address the root problem?
- **Execution:** Does the main demo flow work?
- **Evidence:** Have assumptions been tested?
- **Technical quality:** Is the implementation appropriate and maintainable?
- **Impact:** Is there a credible path to useful outcomes?
- **Presentation:** Can the team explain the project and its limitations?
